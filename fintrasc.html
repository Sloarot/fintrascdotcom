WHY IT MATTERS

Why you can't just ask AI...

Translating regulated financial documents is not simply a linguistic exercise. Inconsistent terminology, imprecise wording or a lack of regulatory awareness can create unnecessary compliance risk.

Yes, AI and machine translation apps can do a lot, we also use them! But for highly technical documents like annual reports, financial statements, or regulatory documents, it's best not to rely solely on computers.

Just think of the many ways the word "rate" can be translated without context! Human translators will always play a crucial part. And that's where we come in...
