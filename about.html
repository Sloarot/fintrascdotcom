About FINTRASC

Specialist translation services for investment funds and financial institutions

What I Do

I help investment funds and financial market participants translate key regulatory and investor-facing documents, including:

Fund Documents

Fund prospectuses and supplements

Investor Information

Key Investor Information Documents (KIIDs) and Key Information Documents (KIDs)

Financial Reports

Annual and semi-annual reports

SFDR Disclosures

SFDR and sustainability disclosures

ESG Documentation

ESG policies and related documentation

My focus: Producing translations that are clear, precise and suitable for regulatory review.

A Compliance-Focused Approach

I work with a strong understanding of the European regulatory environment for investment funds, including:

UCITS and AIFMD frameworks

PRIIPs requirements

SFDR and EU sustainability disclosures

While I do not provide legal advice, my translations reflect the regulatory context in which these documents are used. Terminology is handled carefully, numerical information is checked thoroughly, and wording is aligned with common market and regulatory practice.

The result is translations designed to stand up to scrutiny.

ESG & Sustainability Disclosures

Sustainability and ESG disclosures require particular care. Ambiguous or promotional language can expose funds to regulatory and reputational risk.

I translate sustainability disclosures with a conservative, accuracy-first approach

Ensuring that ESG terminology is used consistently and in line with regulatory expectations, while faithfully reflecting the source text.

This supports credible and compliant sustainability reporting across languages.
